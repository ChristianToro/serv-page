The problem

  1. Messages come in from everywhere.

    Texts, social DMs, calls. Each one is a job, and each one is easy to miss.

  2. Replies wait until your hands are free.

    By the time you write back, the customer may have gone somewhere else.

  3. A paper calendar can’t say no.

    It will let you book the same Saturday twice.

Plain English

People keep telling you to “get AI.” Here’s what that actually means.

It means a helper that answers the easy questions for you, instantly, at any hour: “Are you open Saturday?”, “How do I book?”, “How much is a tune-up?” It replies in your shop’s words and hands anything tricky back to you. No robots, no new gadgets. It works with the phone and social page you already use.

Services

  1. 01

    One inbox, instant reply

    Texts and social messages land in one place. Every customer gets an immediate reply that says you got their message and how to book. Common questions get answered on the spot. Anything unusual comes to you.

  2. 02

    Online booking

    Customers pick an open time themselves, from their phone. Once a slot is taken, it disappears, so the same Saturday can’t be sold twice. You see the whole schedule on your phone.

  3. 03

    Setup, training, support

    I set everything up for you. Then I show you and anyone who works the counter how to use it, at your shop, until it feels easy. If it isn’t getting used, it isn’t finished, and I keep helping.
